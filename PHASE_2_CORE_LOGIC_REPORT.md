## Business and data rules

- **Checkout:** COD only. Full name, `01[3–9]` Bangladesh mobile number, street address, district, area and a serving zone are required; email, four-digit postal code and notes are optional. At most 50 input lines and 100 units per SKU after duplicate lines are combined. An authenticated buyer must be an active customer; delivery details are never proof of identity. The store minimum applies to pre-discount merchandise. Payable = merchandise subtotal − discount + the configured zone fee. Shipping is not discountable. The order and line snapshots, not cart prices, feed downstream screens.
- **Retries and Phase 1 access:** A normalized request fingerprint and authenticated `Order.userId` or server-issued anonymous checkout scope must match. A guest receives a distinct, random HttpOnly checkout cookie (`SameSite=Lax`, Secure in production, 30 days); its digest binds retries. The guest receipt token is derived for that key from the separate secret and can be redelivered only with the original expiry. The receipt still requires the per-receipt-path cookie, matching digest, anonymous order and unexpired grant (30 minutes); the checkout cookie, order number, phone or UUID alone cannot open it. No grant is renewed. Account reads remain owner-only and do not fall back to guest grants. Legacy orders without a fingerprint cannot replay through the new checkout.
- **Variants:** New version-2 orders debit the variant for a variant line and the product for a simple line. A product with any variant records, even if all are archived, cannot fall back to selling base stock. A missing variant price falls back to base price; variant SKU falls back to product SKU. Edits carry stock and membership baselines so stale forms cannot silently overwrite a changed stock value or archive an unseen variant. Historical version-1 orders used both parent and variant debits for variant lines; valid version-1 cancellations restore both. Missing or ambiguous historical references block the entire cancellation and require operator review.
- **Coupons:** Codes normalize to uppercase. Empty eligibility lists mean storewide; otherwise a line qualifies by matching a product or one of its active categories, counted at most once. The coupon minimum applies to the whole merchandise subtotal. A percentage is capped at 100%; percentage discounts round half-up to the paisa and all discounts are capped by eligible merchandise value. New expiry dates include the full specified Bangladesh day. Each successful order uses one slot, with at most one redemption per authenticated account or guest checkout cookie. Cancelling or returning does not release a slot. Guest repeat-use enforcement is browser-scoped, not a verified person/phone limit; clearing cookies or using a different browser changes the scope. Historic `usedCount` remains; per-buyer redemptions are not backfilled.
- **Fulfillment and cancellation:** `PENDING → CONFIRMED → PACKED → SHIPPED → DELIVERED`. Admins may cancel unpaid pending, confirmed or packed orders; authenticated owners may cancel unpaid pending or confirmed orders only. Guests can contact support to request cancellation; no public cancellation credential was added. `SHIPPED` and `DELIVERED` may transition to `RETURNED`; returns do not automatically restore stock. `CANCELLED` and `RETURNED` are terminal for fulfillment. A guarded state change, restoration and audit event all roll back together if any step fails. Admin order detail shows recent status and payment activity.
- **COD:** Only admins can record full COD collection after delivery, correct a delivered paid order to unpaid, or record a completed refund on a returned paid order. **Order value** excludes cancelled and returned orders; **pending COD** is the unpaid/failed COD value for non-cancelled, non-returned orders, including delivered but unpaid orders. **Collected COD** is recorded paid on delivered orders, including previously delivered returns until refunded. Cancelled, unpaid and refunded orders never count as collected. Metrics are a current-state view of orders placed in the selected date range, not a ledger by cash receipt date, a courier remittance report or proof of bank settlement.

## Migration, transactions and query review

- `prisma/migrations/20261120163000_core_checkout_integrity/migration.sql` is additive: zone district and coupon scope arrays, variant active flag, coupon redemption composite primary key and FK, nullable checkout fingerprints and guest scopes, legacy inventory version default, `(userId, placedAt)` index, and two `CHECK (stock >= 0) NOT VALID` constraints. No update, delete, relink or backfill of existing customer, order, payment or receipt records. Old negative stock remains visible for review; the constraints prevent new negative writes.
- Checkout and status commands run in serializable Prisma transactions with bounded retries for PostgreSQL write conflicts/deadlocks. Checkout also retries the identified unique order number/key conflict. The replay lookup runs again in the retry; it does not trust uniqueness alone as authorization. Conditional stock writes require sufficient quantity and the right active SKU. Coupon use has a conditional capacity/expiry predicate and a composite uniqueness check. Cancellation compares previous fulfillment and payment status, then restores exact snapshot references and records its audit event.
- Quote reads are bounded by cart size and load the requested variant rows rather than every option. Checkout lookups use existing primary/unique indexes for products, zones, codes, keys and account associations. Restoration uses the order-item order ID index; activity uses the existing audit entity/ID index and a 30-event limit. COD headline metrics use grouped SQL sums in a consistent snapshot; low-stock lists compare thresholds before `LIMIT`. The deployment data volume is unknown; no production query plan or load benchmark was run. Item/category breakdowns and CSV exports still read matching rows, and hot coupon/SKU contention can exhaust bounded retries under heavy load.

## Rollout and remaining limitations

1. Rehearse `npm run db:deploy` and regenerate Prisma Client on staging. Apply the additive migration before serving the new build. Review migration/index lock timing on the target table size. Do not run the demo seed on existing environments. Avoid mixed old/new checkout and fulfillment writers: old code does not understand version-2 reservations. If rollback is needed after new orders exist, pause order writes and use a compatible forward fix or verified rollback plan.
2. **Configure districts in Admin → Settings → Delivery Zones before reopening checkout.** Legacy zones have empty district lists and serve nobody until mapped. Review overlapping zones/charges and test served and unserved districts. Demo seed maps Dhaka and the other listed districts for fresh demo data only. Confirm the COD toggle and minimum order amount.
3. Read-only review legacy stock and references (negative stocks, missing variant IDs/products and suspected prior partial cancellations) before operating on those orders. Do not automatically repair, reassign or backfill old records. Validate old coupon thresholds, expiry dates and used counts against operator records. Verify the deployed receipt path/cache protections over HTTPS with separate guest and account sessions.
4. In staging, exercise review/confirm with a price change, a dropped response and retry, final coupon slot/stock, archived variant cancellation, and paid/refunded reporting. Enter a second order only after checking an ambiguous first outcome. Retry recovery relies on the original tab intent/key and buyer scope; different devices, edited inputs or cleared session/cookies may not be recoverable. Track Order or support remains the fallback, not a receipt or ownership grant.
5. Still out of scope: saved-address checkout selection, geographic address verification, deliberate cross-browser coupon abuse prevention, post-delivery inspection/restock, partial payments/refunds or courier settlement reconciliation, historical payment verification, variant-aware catalog price filtering/sort, and broader audit fixes. Item margin excludes discounts, delivery, operating expenses and missing costs; category totals may overlap by design. Receipt/account privacy is not a production-readiness sign-off.
